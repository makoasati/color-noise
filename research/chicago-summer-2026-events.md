## READ FIRST: Where the pictures actually are

**Context:** this is a class project, so image use is treated as academic/educational here rather than commercial publishing. The section below is therefore organized around **where to find the best 2026 photos fast**, with credit lines you can paste.

**Two practical notes, not legal lectures:**

1. **This repo is wired to a live Vercel project (`color-noise`).** Coursework submitted to an instructor and a site deployed at a public URL are different things in practice. If it stays local / preview-only / unlisted for grading, the sourcing below is fine as-is. If you point a real domain at it and it gets traffic, swap the Getty-sourced images for the 🟢 free ones — everything below is labeled so you can do that in one pass later without re-researching.
2. **Credit everything anyway — for the grade, not the lawyers.** An uncredited photo in a class project is a *citation* problem. Every entry below has a ready-made credit line. The `events` table already has `primary_source_url` / `primary_source_name` / `additional_sources` columns; use them for image credits too and you get a bibliography for free.

### Cover images — one per article

Every one of the 86 entries now carries a **🖼️ COVER IMAGE** line: a single designated pick for the `events.image_url` column, followed by **Alternates**.

**25 of them are direct, free, hotlinkable URLs** in this form:
`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/FILENAME.jpg`
That path 301-redirects to the real `upload.wikimedia.org` file, so it works straight in an `<img src>`. Every filename was verified to exist via the Commons API — **none are guessed**. Still open each file page once to read its exact licence (CC BY-SA vs CC0 vs PD) before publishing.

**The honest constraint:** free, openly-licensed photographs *of the actual 2026 event* barely exist. So covers fall into three kinds:
1. 🟢 **Free event photo** — rare and excellent. **#37 Chicago Pride Parade is the jackpot:** Commons holds a full set of real 2026 parade photos. #1 Lollapalooza has exactly one (Tate McRae). #66 Air & Water has several, plus public-domain military imagery.
2. 🟢 **Free thematic/venue shot** — the workhorse, and what most publications actually use for listings. Often *better* than an event photo: the Paseo Boricua steel flag (#41) carries the displacement story, the Pilsen immigrants mural (#42) is thematically exact *and* face-free, the Tiffany dome (#86) does all the work by itself.
3. 🟡 **One email away** — a named social account or photo essay, with the specific frame to request. Each says *why* that source.

**Two things to know:**
- For the four immigration-enforcement articles (#42, #43, #44, #55) I deliberately chose covers with **no identifiable faces** — murals, monuments, livestream stills. That constraint drove the pick, not aesthetics.
- **#72 World Music Festival runs through Oct 4, 2026.** Its cover is: go shoot it. That's the one chance in this whole package for an original, exclusive image.

### Legend used throughout

| Tag | Meaning |

| 🟢 **FREE** | Public domain, Creative Commons, or an official press kit — reusable anywhere, including a public site |

### The fastest routes to good images

**1. Choose Chicago Image & Video Library — start here every time**
https://www.choosechicago.com/press-media/image-and-video-library/
Read agreement → check box → you're in. Free, high-resolution, covers most major city festivals.
- Credit exactly: `[Photographer Name]/[Collection Name]/Choose Chicago`
- They can pull individual images, so **download and keep local copies** rather than hotlinking

**2. Official festival press kits and recap pages — where the 2026 headliner shots live**
Per-event contacts are in each entry. Standouts:
- **Riot Fest** — the single best-stocked event here. Official galleries at https://riotfest.org/category/photos/ plus a crowd-sourced pool, "Photos By You! Riot Fest 2026," at https://riotfest.org/2026/09/29/photos-by-you-riot-fest-2026/ where the photographers already agreed to republication.
- **Lollapalooza** — `press@c3presents.com`. Assets are editorial-use with festival name + photographer credit.
- **Insomniac / Beyond Wonderland** and **Ravinia** both run generous, well-organized press libraries. Ravinia's includes architecture photography of the new $75M Hunter Pavilion.
- **Renegade Craft** has a genuinely excellent press image library.
- Several festivals posted their own 2026 recap galleries — **Sueños** (https://suenosmusicfestival.com/suenos-2026-recap/) is the best example.

**3. US military public affairs — 🟢 free, high-res, public domain**
For the **Air & Water Show (#66)**: Thunderbirds and Golden Knights imagery is US Government work and generally public domain. Pull from **DVIDS** (dvidshub.net) and Air Force / Army public affairs sites. Best free-art situation in the dossier.

**4. Wikimedia Commons — 🟢 but thinner than you'd hope**
Verified today:
- `Category:Lollapalooza` — **1 file** in the main category (*Tate McRae Live at Lollapalooza 2026.jpg*), plus `Lollapalooza by year` (20 subcats) and `by country` (5)
- `Category:Lollapalooza_2026` — **does not exist (404)**
- `Category:Riot_Fest` — exists

- `Category:Millennium_Park,_Chicago` — well populated
- Also strong for **historical** material: Maxwell Street (#61), Paseo Boricua's steel flags (#41), Bud Billiken's Chicago Defender origins (#45)

**Use Commons for venue, streetscape and archival shots. It will not give you 2026 performance photos.**

**5. Flickr, license-filtered — 🟢**
https://www.flickr.com/creativecommons/ — filter to CC BY or CC BY-SA. The **Chicago Reader Music pool** (https://www.flickr.com/groups/chicago_reader_music/) is where local concert photographers post; licenses vary per photo, but it's also the best place to *find* a Chicago shooter if you ever want original work.

**6. Local outlets — 🟡, and unusually approachable**
For a student project these are likely yeses, and they have the 2026 photos the wire services don't:
- **The TRiiBE** — Chosen Few Picnic, Silver Room, Bud Billiken, Juneteenth, Pride South Side
- **5 Magazine** — house music: Chosen Few, West Fest, Chicago House Music Festival
- **Block Club Chicago** — neighborhood fests citywide (articles are CC BY-ND 4.0; **whether that covers staff photos is unconfirmed — just ask**)
- **Hyde Park Herald**, **Newcity**, **Third Coast Review**, **Latination**, **WDCB** — all sent people into the field in 2026

**7. Getty / AP / news galleries — 🟠 CLASS-OK**
Most of the striking 2026 festival imagery surfaced by search is Getty. For coursework, cite it (`Photographer/Getty Images`) and move on. Flag these in your image log so a future public launch is a find-and-replace, not a re-shoot. Same for Instagram — `@suenosfestival`, `@northcoastfest`, `@westfestchicago`, `@africanfestchi`, `@budbillikenparade`, `@chicagodcase` all have strong 2026 galleries.

### Two notes that survive any use context

- **DCASE has no public photo library** that I could find — `flickr.com/photos/chicagodcase` is a **404**. The City of Chicago also states it "neither grants nor enforces copyright," so a city press-release image belongs to the individual photographer. For city festivals (#18–24), Choose Chicago is the reliable route; email DCASE for a photographer's name if you need a specific shot.
- **Identifiable crowd faces at the 2026 Latino community events (#42, #43, #44, #55).** This is not a copyright question and it does not relax for a class project. Those festivals happened under active federal immigration enforcement; publishing a recognizable face from them online can expose a real person. **Use wide shots, stage shots, or livestream stills.** It costs you nothing and it's the one image rule here worth treating as firm.

### Workflow per article
1. Choose Chicago library
2. Event press kit or 2026 recap gallery
3. Commons / Flickr-CC for venue and archival
4. Local outlet ask for anything specific
5. Getty or news gallery as the 🟠 fallback, logged for later replacement
6. Record photographer, source, credit string, license tag and pull date for every image — that's your bibliography and your future swap list
